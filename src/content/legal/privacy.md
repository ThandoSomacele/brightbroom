This Privacy Policy explains how {{brand.legalName}} ("**{{brand.name}}**", "**we**", "**us**") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use {{brand.domain}}, book a clean, work as a cleaner, or represent a cleaning company on our platform. It is written to meet the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("**POPIA**").

## 1. Who we are

1.1 {{brand.name}} runs an online marketplace that connects customers with independent cleaning companies ("**Cleaning Partners**"). For the personal information described in this policy, {{brand.name}} is the "responsible party" under POPIA.

1.2 Our Information Officer is {{brand.informationOfficer}}, who can be contacted at {{brand.supportEmail}}, {{brand.supportPhone}}, or {{brand.address}}.

1.3 Cleaning Partners are separate businesses. When we share a customer's booking details with a Cleaning Partner so that it can do the job, the Cleaning Partner becomes responsible for how it uses that information, and our [Cleaning Partner Agreement](/partner-terms) requires it to protect the information and use it only for that booking. Each Cleaning Partner is also responsible for the personal information of its own employees.

## 2. Who this policy applies to

This policy covers:

1. **customers**, who book cleans, including guests who book without an account;
2. **cleaners**, who apply to work through {{brand.name}} or are added by a Cleaning Partner;
3. **people who represent Cleaning Partners**, such as owners, directors and administrators; and
4. **visitors** to our website and people who contact us.

## 3. What we collect

| Who | Information | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Customers | Name, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a secure hash), service addresses with map coordinates, access instructions and notes, booking history, recurring schedules, add-ons and preferences, messages to us and feedback about cleans | You, when you register, book or contact us |
| Customers | Payment records: amount, date, status, payment method type and PayFast reference. We do not receive or store full card or bank account numbers | PayFast |
| Cleaners | Name, contact details, ID or passport number, work authorisation and its expiry, tax number, home area, work radius and availability, experience and training, profile photo, application documents, bank details for payment, ratings and job history | The cleaner, or the Cleaning Partner that adds them |
| Cleaning Partner representatives | Name, contact details, role in the company, company registration documents, a director's identity document, proof of business address, bank confirmation letter and bank details | The representative, when the company signs up |
| Website visitors | IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed and how the site is used, cookie choices | Your browser (see section 9) |

3.1 We do not intentionally collect special personal information (such as health, religion, or criminal records), and we do not process the personal information of children. Please do not include such information in booking notes.

3.2 Identity and work-permit information about cleaners, and director identity documents, are collected because the law requires employers to confirm a person's right to work and because we must be able to verify who we are dealing with.

## 4. Why we use it

We process personal information only where POPIA allows it. The table shows our main purposes and the reason each is lawful.

| Purpose | Lawful reason (POPIA section 11) |
|---|---|
| Creating and running your account | Needed to perform our contract with you |
| Taking bookings, assigning a Cleaning Partner and cleaner, and sending confirmations, reminders and updates | Needed to perform our contract with you |
| Collecting payments, issuing receipts and refunds, and paying Cleaning Partners | Contract; legal obligation (tax and accounting records) |
| Vetting Cleaning Partners and cleaners, including identity, right-to-work and bank verification | Legitimate interest in a safe, trustworthy platform; legal obligation |
| Handling complaints, damage claims and disputes | Legitimate interest; establishing or defending legal claims |
| Keeping the website secure, preventing fraud and spam, and limiting abuse | Legitimate interest |
| Understanding how the website is used so we can improve it | Your consent (analytics cookies) |
| Sending news and offers about our own services | Your consent, or, for existing customers, our legitimate interest under POPIA section 69(3). You can opt out at any time |

## 5. Who we share it with

5.1 **Cleaning Partners and cleaners.** When a booking is assigned, we share what is needed to do the job: the customer's name, phone number, the service address and access instructions, and the booking details. Cleaners see the customer's first name, the address, and the booking instructions. Cleaning Partners also see their own cleaners' details and their bookings and payouts.

5.2 **Customers.** Customers see their assigned cleaner's first name, photo and rating.

5.3 **Service providers ("operators")** who process information on our behalf under written agreements that require them to keep it secure and confidential:

| Provider | What they do | Where the data is processed |
|---|---|---|
| Netlify | Hosts the website and runs our server code | United States and global edge network |
| Neon | Hosts our database | United Kingdom (London) |
| Amazon Web Services (S3) | Stores uploaded documents and photos | South Africa (Cape Town) |
| PayFast | Processes payments | South Africa |
| Resend | Sends our emails | United States |
| Google Maps Platform | Address search, map display and distance calculations | United States and global |
| PostHog | Website analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies | European Union |

5.4 **Others, where necessary:** our professional advisers (such as attorneys, auditors and insurers); law enforcement, courts and regulators where the law requires it or to protect someone's safety; and a buyer or successor of all or part of our business, who must continue to protect the information as this policy describes.

5.5 We do not sell personal information.

## 6. Information sent outside South Africa

6.1 Some of our service providers process information outside South Africa (see section 5.3). We transfer information only where POPIA section 72 allows it: where the recipient is bound by law, binding corporate rules or a binding agreement that gives protection substantially similar to POPIA, or where the transfer is needed to perform our contract with you.

6.2 Identity documents, work permits, company documents and photos uploaded to {{brand.name}} are stored in South Africa.

## 7. How we protect it

7.1 We use appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

1. encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all traffic to the website;
2. passwords stored only as strong one-way hashes;
3. private document storage, where identity, company and application documents can only be opened through short-lived links issued after checking who is asking (cleaner profile photos are the exception: they are shown publicly so that customers know who to expect);
4. access to personal information limited to the people who need it for their role; and
5. regular backups.

7.2 If we have reasonable grounds to believe that personal information has been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, we will notify the Information Regulator and the people affected as soon as reasonably possible, as POPIA section 22 requires.

7.3 No system is completely secure. Please keep your password private and tell us immediately if you think your account has been compromised.

## 8. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires. As a guide:

| Information | How long |
|---|---|
| Customer accounts and saved addresses | While the account is open, and up to 12 months after the last booking or account closure |
| Booking, payment and payout records | 5 years after the end of the tax year they relate to (tax and accounting law) |
| Cleaner and Cleaning Partner vetting documents | While the cleaner or Cleaning Partner is active on {{brand.name}}, and up to 5 years afterwards in case of claims |
| Applications that are not approved | 12 months after the decision |
| Complaints, damage claims and related messages | Up to 3 years after the matter is closed |
| Website analytics | As long as needed to understand trends, after which it is deleted or anonymised |

After these periods we delete the information or anonymise it so that it no longer identifies anyone.

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

9.1 **Strictly necessary** cookies keep you signed in (`session`), protect forms against forgery (`csrf`) and remember a guest booking while you complete it (`guest_booking_data`). The website cannot work without them.

9.2 **Preference** storage remembers your cookie choice in your browser.

9.3 **Analytics** (PostHog) helps us understand how the website is used, for example which pages are visited and where people get stuck. It is only switched on if you accept analytics cookies, and you can change your choice at any time on our [cookie preferences](/cookie-preferences) page.

9.4 Google Maps, which powers address search, may set its own cookies when you use the address search. Google's privacy policy applies to those.

## 10. Your rights

10.1 Under POPIA you have the right to:

1. ask whether we hold personal information about you and to request a copy of it (see our PAIA manual at [brightbroom.com/paia](https://brightbroom.com/paia));
2. ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained;
3. object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
4. withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting processing that has already taken place; and
5. complain to the Information Regulator.

10.2 To exercise these rights, email {{brand.supportEmail}}. We will need to confirm your identity before we act on a request, and we will respond within a reasonable time. We may refuse a request where the law allows, and we will tell you why.

10.3 Some information cannot be deleted immediately because the law requires us to keep it (for example payment records), or because it is needed for an open complaint or claim.

10.4 **Information Regulator (South Africa).** Website: inforegulator.org.za. Complaints: POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.

## 11. Direct marketing

11.1 We will only send you marketing about our own services if you have agreed to it or, as an existing customer, about similar services, and every marketing message lets you unsubscribe for free.

11.2 Cleaning Partners may not use customer information received through {{brand.name}} to market to customers.
